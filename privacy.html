Privacy Notice

Last updated: 26 September 2026

Guinea Pig Trading respects the commercial sensitivity of buyer enquiries. This notice explains what happens to information submitted through this website or sent directly by email.

01

Information you provide

You may provide your name, company, business email, website, destination, postal code, product interests, budget, quantity, deadline, contact preference and other information included in your enquiry or later correspondence.

02

How the website form works

The enquiry form does not submit information to a website database. It prepares a draft email in your own mail application. You can review, edit or cancel that email before sending it.

03

How information is used

Information is used to assess fit, respond to enquiries, research requested products, prepare quotations, coordinate approved work, maintain commercial records and address legal or accounting obligations.

04

Supplier and service-provider sharing

Only information reasonably necessary for the requested work may be shared with prospective suppliers, carriers, payment providers or professional advisers. Confidential buyer details are not published as case studies or testimonials without permission.

05

Retention and security

Business correspondence and transaction records may be retained for the period reasonably required to manage the relationship, resolve questions and meet record-keeping obligations. Reasonable care is used, but no email or internet system can be guaranteed completely secure.

06

Analytics and cookies

This website does not intentionally set advertising or behavioral-tracking cookies. It is hosted through GitHub Pages, whose infrastructure may process standard technical request data according to GitHub’s own policies.

07

Your choices

You may ask what personal information is held in active business records, request correction, or request deletion where retention is not required for contractual, legal or record-keeping purposes.
