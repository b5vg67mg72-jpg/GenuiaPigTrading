Commercial Terms

Last updated: 26 September 2026

These principles explain the normal working approach of Guinea Pig Trading / Guinea Pig Japan Sourcing. They are not a substitute for the written scope, quotation or invoice issued for a specific engagement. Where a specific written agreement differs, that agreement governs the relevant work.

01

Enquiries and scope

An initial enquiry and fit review are free. Research, sourcing, purchasing coordination, inspection support, packing or logistics work begins only after the scope, deliverables, timing and any applicable fee have been confirmed in writing.

02

Research and sourcing fees

No universal fee applies to every assignment. If custom research or a shortlist is chargeable, the fee and whether it is refundable or creditable against later work are stated before work starts. Completed research work is generally not refundable merely because a buyer decides not to order.

03

Quotations and estimates

Unless explicitly stated otherwise, product availability, prices, freight, exchange rates and lead times are estimates until reconfirmed. Product, service, Japan domestic freight, packing, international transport, insurance and payment charges are separated where practical.

04

Purchasing authority

A general enquiry, research engagement or product shortlist does not authorize a purchase. Goods are purchased only after the buyer approves the applicable written quotation, quantities and terms. Substitutions or material budget changes require additional approval.

05

Payment

Currency, payment method, due date and payee details are provided on the applicable quotation or invoice. Guinea Pig Trading does not collect payment-card data through this website. Orders may require cleared funds before supplier purchasing begins.

06

Stock changes and cancellations

Japanese market availability can change without notice. If an approved item becomes unavailable before purchase, the buyer will be offered a revised option, substitution for approval, or an appropriate adjustment for the unpurchased item. Supplier cancellation rules and non-refundable third-party costs may limit refunds after funds are committed.

07

Authenticity and licensed goods

Unclear-origin or suspected counterfeit goods are not knowingly proposed. For licensed goods, source, authenticity indicators, availability and known resale conditions are reviewed before formal quotation. This service does not represent official brand authorization or guarantee that a product may be resold in every country or channel.

08

Shipping, damage and insurance

Freight estimates may change after actual packing. Carrier service, insurance and declared-value arrangements are agreed for the specific shipment. Visible damage or shortage should be documented promptly with photographs of the outer carton, labels, internal packing and affected goods so any available carrier or supplier process can be assessed.

09

Import responsibility

The buyer is responsible for confirming destination-country import, product-safety, labeling, intellectual-property, tax and resale requirements. Customs duties, import taxes, brokerage and destination charges are the buyer’s responsibility unless expressly included in writing.
