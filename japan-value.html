Make the numbers work for your store

A price gap is the starting point.

Convert both prices using the exchange rate available to your business. Then include Japan delivery, sourcing fees, packing, international freight, duties, non-recoverable taxes, payment charges and a provision for returns. For a resale decision, also deduct marketplace fees, local fulfilment and any discounts from your expected selling price.

Estimated contribution per unit = expected net sales proceeds − total landed cost.

Published asking prices do not prove demand, completed sales or achievable resale value. Check competing local offers and sales evidence. Supplier resale conditions and destination requirements must also fit your order.
