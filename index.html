Who we are

Built from an international perspective, based in Japan.

I’m Lizzy, the founder of Guinea Pig Japan Sourcing. Having lived in New Zealand, China and several other countries before making Japan my home, I understand how differently buyers, customers and markets can think. I created this business to use that cross-cultural experience to connect people and opportunities around the world.

Every buyer’s requirement is different. Whether you have a precise product request or only an idea of what may suit your customers, we listen carefully, assess what is realistically possible and build a sourcing approach around your market, budget and priorities.

The business is now developing in several directions. Alongside helping international buyers source from Japan, my longer-term aim is to identify thoughtful products from overseas and introduce them to suitable audiences in Japan—creating a genuine two-way exchange.

Commercial FAQ

Key points before an engagement.

Is there a fixed minimum order?

No single minimum applies across all products. Supplier MOQ, case pack and channel requirements differ. An estimated quantity or budget is needed to identify realistic options.

Can stock be guaranteed?

No. Japanese retail and wholesale availability can change quickly. Current stock is checked as close as practical to the approved purchasing stage.

Can licensed character products be sourced?

Potentially, when authenticity, supply source and resale conditions can be checked. A licensed product is not automatically cleared for every destination or sales channel.

Can I request a candidate list before ordering?

Yes. A current candidate list can be researched around your buying brief. The research depth and any applicable service scope are agreed before work begins.

How are shipping costs handled?

Options depend on dimensions, weight, value and destination. An estimate is provided before purchasing; final cost may change after actual packing.
