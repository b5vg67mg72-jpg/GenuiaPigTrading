Who I am

Hi, I’m Lizzy. I live and work in Japan.

Before settling here, I lived in New Zealand, China and a few other countries. Moving around taught me to listen, ask questions and never assume that one market works like another.

I started Guinea Pig Trading because I enjoy connecting people with things they might not find on their own. I now help overseas shops research Japanese products, compare their options and organise purchases without losing sight of the budget or practical details.

Commercial FAQ

Key points before an engagement.

Is there a fixed minimum order?

No single minimum applies across all products. Supplier MOQ, case pack and channel requirements differ. An estimated quantity or budget is needed to identify realistic options.

Can I request a candidate list before ordering?

Yes. A current candidate list can be researched around your buying brief. The research depth and any applicable service scope are agreed before work begins.

How are shipping costs handled?

Options depend on dimensions, weight, value and destination. An estimate is provided before purchasing; final cost may change after actual packing.
