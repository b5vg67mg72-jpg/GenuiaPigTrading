Commercial information

Practical answers
before engagement.

Will we have a written agreement?01

Yes. Before an approved purchase, the project scope, products, costs, payment stages, responsibilities and order-specific change or cancellation terms are recorded in a written sourcing agreement or order confirmation for both sides to review.

Who will I actually work with?02

You work directly with Guinea Pig, an independent sourcing coordinator based in Japan. The same person handles your brief, research, quotation and order follow-through.

Are the catalog products in stock?03

Not automatically. The catalog shows real products and official reference information. Current stock, purchase limits, MOQ and export suitability are checked when you send an inquiry.

Where can products be sourced from?04

The route depends on the product and quantity. Research may include makers, authorized retailers, distributors, wholesalers and specialist Japan-side stores. A listing is never presented as a wholesale relationship unless that has been confirmed.

How will I understand the price?05

Your proposal separates the product cost, sourcing or handling service, Japan-side delivery and estimated international shipping wherever practical. Official retail prices on this site are references, not promised supply prices.
