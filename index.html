Buyer fit

Best for specialist retailers.

A quick fit check saves both sides time and leads to better product proposals.

A good fit

  • Independent toy, anime, collectible or gift retailers
  • Buyers seeking Japan-only or hard-to-find small goods
  • Stores able to provide a target price and quantity range
  • Businesses comfortable approving terms in writing
  • Retailers looking for repeat sourcing support

Please note

  • This is independent sourcing, not an official brand distributorship
  • No inventory or availability is guaranteed before verification
  • Counterfeit, grey-market or unclear-origin goods are not proposed
  • Food, matcha and Japanese knives are outside the current scope
  • Marketplace account creation or resale compliance remains the buyer’s responsibility

Buyer questions

Clear answers before you commit.

Is there a fixed minimum order?

Not across every product. Minimums depend on the supplier, case pack and channel. Share an estimated quantity or budget so realistic options can be researched.

Can you guarantee stock?

No. Japanese retail and wholesale availability can change quickly. Current stock is confirmed as close as practical to the approved purchasing stage.

Do you supply licensed character products?

Potentially, when authenticity, supply source and resale conditions can be checked. A product bearing a licence does not automatically mean it is cleared for every destination or sales channel.

Can I request only a product list?

Yes. A current candidate list can be prepared around your brief. Quotation depth and research scope are agreed before work begins.

How is shipping handled?

Shipping options depend on dimensions, weight, value and destination. Estimates are provided before purchasing; final costs may change after actual packing.
